Where jewellery meets excellence

As a gemologist, I’m driven by the meeting point of natural wonder and advanced gemological science, transforming raw stones into stories.

Dalal Ahmed Habib is an accomplished Emirati jewelry design specialist, consultant, and GIA Gemologist, with an impressive professional trajectory spanning 18 years in marketing and communications. A passionate artisan with an exceptional eye for detail, Dalal has carved a niche for herself in the industry, achieving a remarkable reputation for her mastery of both craft and communication.


Shifting her career focus, Dalal ventured into independent consultancy for high-profile international jewelry brands. Her expertise in marketing and communications, combined with her specialized knowledge in gems and jewelry design, has facilitated the creation of unique, exquisite pieces that perfectly align with the needs and tastes of a discerning global clientele.


In her quest for continued growth and excellence, Dalal pursued and obtained prestigious diplomas in gemology and jewelry specialization from GIA London, underscoring her commitment to staying abreast of industry standards and innovation.


Dalal's work is characterized by her innovative approach to traditional jewelry design, always pushing the boundaries with unyielding creativity and diligence. This is reflected in the effective marketing strategies she develops for their promotion.


Behind the scenes, Dalal is an adept business woman, who places client satisfaction at the forefront of her priorities. Her combined expertise in design, gemology, marketing, commitment to client satisfaction, and her distinct eye for detail, has earned her a loyal following of clients, a testament to her success in creating not only stunning jewelry but also lasting relationships.
